## 🎬 LinkedIn Video Script

**Format:** Screen Recording
**Audio:** An upbeat, modern, lo-fi tech track in the background (keep the volume low).

#### **0:00 - 0:05 | The Hook (The Problem)**
- **Visual:** Start on a standard, boring PDF resume open on your screen. Shake the mouse cursor over it slightly to show frustration.
- **Voiceover:** "Tired of sending resumes into the void and getting zero feedback? Let’s fix that." 

#### **0:05 - 0:15 | The Solution (The "Wow" Moment)**
- **Visual:** Cut to the ElevateAI Landing Page. Zoom in quickly on the primary Call-to-Action button. Show you dragging and dropping a PDF file into the upload zone.
- **Voiceover:** "Meet ElevateAI. Upload your resume and let Generative AI analyze it in seconds."

#### **0:15 - 0:25 | The Magic (The Core Feature)**
- **Visual:** Show the loading animation briefly. Then, **BAM**! Cut to the results page showing the **Resume Score**. Scroll down smoothly to show the parsed data and the categorized feedback. 
- **Voiceover:** "Built with the Gemini API, it scores your resume and highlights exactly what’s missing—from weak action verbs to missing keywords."

#### **0:25 - 0:35 | The Deep-Dive (Engineering Value)**
- **Visual:** Click on the "Enhance" or "Tailor" feature. Watch the AI generate a much stronger, elite-level bullet point right on the screen. 
- **Voiceover:** "It doesn't just grade you. It gives you elite-level, tailored rewrites to make your experience stand out to ATS systems."

#### **0:35 - 0:45 | The Flex (Showing off the Architecture & UI)**
- **Visual:** In quick successions: Click on the Support/Feedback Widget, then show a quick screen recording of your VS Code backend (briefly show the `exponential backoff` or `retry` logic in your Java code).
- **Voiceover:** "Backed by a robust Spring Boot backend with error retry logic, and a seamless Vite/React frontend."

#### **0:45 - 0:50 | The Call to Action (Outro)**
- **Visual:** The screen fades to the project's URL in bold text, along with your LinkedIn handle. 
- **Voiceover:** "Ready to elevate your job search? Try it live. Link in the comments below!"
